# AI Business Launch Kits

Seven free Markdown kits for building an AI service business, one step at a time, with the AI assistant you already use.

Created by Desmond Dixon for the **AI Mentorship for World Travelers** podcast.

## How to use them

1. Open a capable AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or similar).
2. Upload or paste one kit.
3. Say: **"Start this kit with me. Ask one question at a time."**
4. Answer honestly, review what it writes, and edit the final document yourself.

Full instructions: [00-how-to-use-the-kits.md](00-how-to-use-the-kits.md)

## Ground rules

- The kits help you think and produce documents. They do not contact prospects, buy software, connect services, or publish anything for you.
- Remove private or client information before sharing your outputs.
- Nothing here promises income, clients, or results. The work is yours.
