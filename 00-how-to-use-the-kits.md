# How to use the AI implementation kits

These files turn each classroom lesson into a guided working session with an AI assistant. Use the
kit that matches the lesson you just completed.

## Quick start

1. Download the Markdown file attached to the lesson.
2. Open ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Codex, or another capable AI assistant.
3. Upload the file to a new conversation.
4. Send this message: `Start this kit with me. Ask one question at a time.`
5. Answer honestly. Type `UNKNOWN` when you do not know something.
6. Review and edit the final deliverable before using or publishing it.
7. Post the approved version in the Build in Public category for feedback.

## Protect private information

Never upload passwords, API keys, bank information, borrower information, personal identifying
information, confidential client files, private contracts, or restricted partner terms. Replace
sensitive details with neutral placeholders such as `[CLIENT]`, `[AMOUNT]`, or `[SYSTEM]`.

## What the AI should and should not do

The AI should ask focused questions, expose assumptions, explain tradeoffs, create a useful first
draft, and help you improve it. It should not invent customer evidence, claim work was completed,
guarantee results, contact people, publish content, or make purchases without your review and
explicit approval.

Treat every output as a draft that requires human judgment. Verify facts, links, calculations,
legal claims, and technical instructions before relying on them.

## Recommended sequence

1. Choose Your AI Path
2. Offer and Ideal Client Builder
3. Minimum Infrastructure Architect
4. Credible Brand Builder
5. Client Acquisition Builder
6. Service Delivery and Proof Builder
7. 30 Day Action Plan Builder

Complete the kits in order unless your situation clearly calls for a different sequence.

## The completion standard

A kit is complete when you have:

- Answered every material question or marked it `UNKNOWN`
- Reviewed the assumptions and risks
- Edited the final output in your own voice
- Removed private or unverified information
- Chosen one concrete next action with a date
- Shared the appropriate deliverable for feedback

Education and mentorship do not guarantee income, clients, or business results. Outcomes depend on
your skills, effort, market, offer quality, and execution.
